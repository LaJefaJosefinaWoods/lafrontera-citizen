Who We Are

La Frontera Citizens is a lawful private community membership network built around culture, service, education, opportunity, humanitarian work, professional development and peaceful community participation.

A Clean Banner

When La Frontera uses the word “cartel,” it is used only as a cultural and branding term describing an organized alliance, network or collective of people and projects.

La Frontera does not promote, participate in, facilitate or associate itself with organized crime, trafficking, extortion, violence, weapons activity, intimidation or other illegal conduct.

What We Stand For

Our mission is to build a documented community where members can connect, participate in lawful opportunities, support community projects, attend events, learn, build businesses and stay informed.

We believe organization should be used to create opportunity, community protection through lawful means, education, accountability and long-term support.

What “La Frontera Citizen” Means

A La Frontera Citizen is an approved member of this private organization. Members may receive a private citizen number, access to member-only updates, events, community programs and other benefits offered by La Frontera.

Important Legal Notice

La Frontera citizenship is a private membership designation only. It does not create or replace United States citizenship, nationality, immigration status, government identification, diplomatic status, tribal status or any government-recognized sovereign status.

Our Community Goals

La Frontera may support lawful projects involving:

Community service • Cultural events • Education • Entrepreneurship • Humanitarian projects • Volunteer work • Professional development • Member opportunities • Community partnerships
